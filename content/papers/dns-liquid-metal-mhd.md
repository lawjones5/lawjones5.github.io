# Direct Numerical Simulation of Turbulent Liquid Metal Flow in Rectangular Ducts under Transverse Magnetic Fields

### Authors:
**Lawrence Jones**, Research Group in Fluid Dynamics and Fusion Engineering, University of Sheffield.

---

## Abstract

Liquid metal magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) governs fluid flow and heat transfer inside fusion reactor breeding blankets. In this study, high-fidelity Direct Numerical Simulations (DNS) were carried out using **CHAPsim2** to examine the suppression of turbulent kinetic energy and the development of boundary layers in electrically conducting fluids under uniform magnetic fields.

We examine Hartmann numbers up to $Ha = 120$ and friction Reynolds numbers $Re_\tau = 400\text{--}800$. The numerical solution demonstrates:
- The thinning of Hartmann boundary layers ($\delta_{Ha} \propto Ha^{-1}$).
- The emergence of characteristic M-shaped velocity profiles in duct cross-sections with conducting side walls.
- Redistribution of Reynolds stress tensors showing near-complete damping of wall-normal velocity fluctuations along the Hartmann walls while quasi-two-dimensional turbulent structures persist parallel to the magnetic field lines.

---

## Key Results & Figures

- **Hartmann Layer Formation**: Quantitative verification of the linear Hartmann scaling and evaluation of secondary Dean-like vortices in duct corners.
- **Pressure Drop Scaling**: Comparison of computed MHD pressure gradients against classic analytical Shercliff and Hunt solutions.
